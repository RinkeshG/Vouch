01 — Brand Essence

Not a tech platform.
A curated magazine.

Vouch doesn't look like a tech product because it isn't trying to be one. It's a place where people curate their favorite spots with the same care a magazine editor curates a feature spread. Every design decision flows from this: warm paper textures over clinical whites, serif type over geometric sans, dashed rules over solid borders, and a rust wax-seal accent that feels stamped by hand.

1
Editorial, not utilitarian
Every screen should feel like a page from a well-designed independent magazine. Generous whitespace, considered typography, deliberate hierarchy. Function through beauty.
2
Warm, not sterile
Paper tones (#F2ECDF) instead of pure white. Warm ink (#181210) instead of pure black. Shadows tinted with sepia. The screen should feel like aged paper, not a hospital wall.
3
Crafted, not assembled
The 2° stamp rotation. Dashed dividers that evoke hand-drawn rules. Caveat annotations that feel written in the margin. Grain texture that breathes life into flat color. Every detail earns its place.
4
Confident, not loud
One accent color (seal rust), not a rainbow. Restrained motion, not gratuitous animation. The brand whispers with authority rather than shouting for attention.
5
Human, not algorithmic
Hand-picked lists, not machine-generated feeds. Personal notes in handwriting font. Author attribution on every piece. The product celebrates individual taste over crowd consensus.

08 — Motion & Interaction

Purposeful movement, never gratuitous.

Every animation serves a purpose: confirming an action, guiding attention, or providing spatial context. No decorative animations. No loading spinners that could be avoided. If it moves, it means something.

Easing Curves

--v-ease-out
cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)
Primary easing. Fast start, gentle deceleration. Used for all standard transitions — hover states, page transitions, element reveals. Feels natural and responsive.
--v-ease-spring
cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)
Spring easing with slight overshoot. Used for stamp animation, place-added bounce, and celebration moments. Creates a playful, physical feel.

09 — Voice & Texture

Whisper, don't shout.

Vouch's personality comes through in three layers: the words we choose, the textures we apply, and the small human touches that make it feel hand-made rather than machine-generated.

Voice & Tone

We sound like
"Name your list, add your spots, share the link. That's it."
  • ✓ Conversational, not corporate
  • ✓ Confident, not boastful
  • ✓ Warm, not saccharine
  • ✓ Brief, not abrupt
We never sound like
"Leverage our platform to optimize your curation experience!"
  • ✗ No jargon or buzzwords
  • ✗ No artificial urgency
  • ✗ No growth-hacker language
  • ✗ No superlatives or claims

Copy Patterns

Page titles Serif, often with italic emphasis "Your taste, curated."
Placeholders Actionable, not decorative "Name your list..." / "Add a place"
Empty states Encouraging, not apologetic "No lists yet" (not "Oops, nothing here!")
CTAs Verb-first, specific "Create your first list" / "Publish"
Errors Clear cause, clear fix "This handle is taken" (not "Error 409")
