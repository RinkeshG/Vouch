# How Vouch becomes a 10x product (not 10% better)

> Document only — the leaps, from first principles. 10x is not more screens; it's
> winning the **decision reflex** (§9: opened before Google/Zomato/WhatsApp) for a
> dense Bengaluru cohort. Each leap below is justified by *why it's a step-change*,
> not a polish.

## The thesis
Aggregators optimise **coverage** (every place, a 4.2★ from strangers). Vouch wins
only if it's **the answer you'd actually act on** — because it carries a *name* and
a *reason* from someone whose taste you've verified. So every 10x lever either
(a) makes the trusted answer **more right**, (b) makes it **closer to action**, or
(c) makes the **graph that powers it grow faster**. Anything else is decoration.

## The six leaps

### 1. Close the loop to ACTION, not a card
Today we stop at "here's the place." The decision isn't done until you've **gone**.
10x = from a trusted answer → *book a table (Resy/■), open directions, split it to
the group, set "going tonight"* — in one tap, with the receipt traveling along.
**Why:** owning the last mile is what makes Vouch the reflex; a recommendation you
don't act on is a bookmark, and bookmarks don't retain.

### 2. The decide-tonight engine (the Search that's missing)
Ask by **occasion × area × time**, get *one confident, open-now, receipted answer*
+ a couple alternates — the CRED-style assisted best action. **Why:** "where do I
go right now?" is the highest-frequency, highest-pain moment; a generic list loses
to Google, but a *trusted single answer* has no incumbent (Delta-4 from zero).
Gated honestly on trust-density so it never ships an empty oracle.

### 3. A supply flywheel that doesn't feel like work
Vouching must be **sub-30-seconds** and *rewarding*: retroactive import ("vouch the
20 places you already love" the first week), the one-line ritual, and guides as the
artifact you're proud to make. **Why:** the graph is the moat, and the graph = vouches.
10x supply → 10x value; the product's growth is its own content (every guide/palate/
vouch is an invite-gated, shareable artifact that recruits the next Knower).

### 4. Real, explainable taste-match (once data is real)
Not the fabricated % we deleted — a *true* overlap that earns a sentence: "you both
live for late-night & filter coffee; she's saved 4 of your spots." **Why:** as the
graph fills, the trust shortcut ("whose recs do I act on?") is the thing no
aggregator can copy — it's *about people*, computed from real behaviour, never a
star average. This is the number that's allowed because it points at humans.

### 5. Own the GROUP decision (the multiplayer wedge)
"The Round" — a shared, link-first decision object your people react to with trust
verbs ("I've been, go"), born to win the group chat. **Why:** the single hardest
real moment is *four people deciding together*; nobody owns it with trust. It's
also the strongest acquisition loop — the decision object leaves the app as a link.

### 6. Become the city's memory (the compounding diary)
Your map + been-diary + guides compound: the more you log, the better your map, the
sharper your palate, the more your guides get borrowed. **Why:** this is the
Letterboxd/Beli flywheel — a personal record that's useful alone on day one and
gets *more* valuable with every entry, making "open Vouch" a habit, not a task.

## What 10x feels like to one user
A Bengalurean opens Vouch on a Friday at 7pm, taps **parents**, and gets *one*
place a friend they trust would stake their name on — open now, 1.2km away, with
the receipt — books it in two taps, sends the Round to the family, and next morning
adds their own vouch. They never opened Google. That reflex, repeated, is the 10x.

## The order of leverage
Backend + the trust graph (so it's real) → the decide-tonight engine (#2) →
close-the-loop-to-action (#1) → the Round (#5) → real taste-match (#4). The diary
(#6) and supply flywheel (#3) compound throughout. Measure only **Useful Decisions
Created**; if a leap doesn't move it, it isn't a leap.
