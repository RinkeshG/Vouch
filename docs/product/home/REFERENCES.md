# Outside-in — how other products solve "the logged-in home"

> The v1 deliberation looked at zero references. This is the corrective: a
> cross-category teardown of real logged-in homes, the **job each home is hired to
> do**, the **cold-start** trick, the **return trigger**, and an explicit
> **transfers / does-NOT-transfer to Vouch** (measured against the Constitution's
> §2 bans: no star scores, no anonymous tiles, no scroll-feed, no map-first, no
> likes, no vanity metrics). Sourced from live research, May 2026.

---

## Food & recommendation

### Beli — *the ranked food diary + taste-similarity*
- **Home shows:** friends' recent spots with scores, your ranked list, Top-10s tuned to you.
- **JTBD:** "Keep my food memory, and tell me where to go via people whose taste is like mine."
- **Cold-start:** log places you've *already been*; rank via **pairwise compare** ("this vs that?") instead of typing a number → a personal 0–10 emerges. Retroactive supply, no network needed.
- **Return:** streaks, friends' new logs, recs sharpen as you log; **taste-similarity score per friend.**
- **Transfers:** (1) **taste-similarity is a number about *people*, not places** — exactly Vouch's allowed taste-match %. (2) **Pairwise ranking** is a no-star way to order your list — fits "no scores" while still ranking privately. (3) Recs-create-habit.
- **Does NOT transfer:** Beli still publishes a **0–10 score per place** — Vouch bans numeric place scores outright. Don't import the public score; import the *private* ranking + the people-similarity.

### The Infatuation — *the situation-first decider*
- **Home shows:** editorial guides + **"Perfect For"** filters (Date, Dinner With The Parents, Late Night, Outdoor).
- **JTBD:** "Decide where to eat for *this specific situation*, trusting a voice."
- **Transfers:** **"Perfect For" == Vouch's occasions** (life-moments, not cuisines) — validates occasion-led home. The explicit **"your restaurant decider"** framing.
- **Does NOT transfer:** trust is a *single editorial authority*, not a personal graph; no people, no receipts. Vouch's wedge is the named friend, not the critic.

### Foursquare / Swarm — *check-in lifelog + declared tastes*
- **Home shows:** chronological check-in lifelog; recs from history + "Tastes."
- **Cold-start:** at onboarding you **pick "Tastes" keywords** (declared prefs) that seed recommendations before you've done anything.
- **Transfers:** **declared-taste onboarding** to warm cold recs; **check-in == the Been stamp** (a lifelog by-product).
- **Does NOT transfer:** the split into two apps fragmented the loop; check-in **coins/stickers/leaderboards became hollow vanity** — Vouch §2 bans vanity. A cautionary tale: gamified logging without a decision payoff decays.

## Taste, library & identity

### Letterboxd — *the diary that doubles as an activity feed*
- **Home shows:** activity from people you follow (watched / liked / listed) + your diary.
- **JTBD:** "Track what I watched, see what my people think, express my taste through lists."
- **Cold-start:** log films you've *already seen* (retroactive supply); the home personalises per user.
- **Transfers:** **diary-as-by-product**, **lists == guides** (identity artifacts), **follow-for-activity**, year-end lists as a re-engagement beat.
- **Does NOT transfer:** it *is* a scroll-feed with ratings — both banned (§2). Take the **diary + lists-as-identity**, leave the feed and the stars.

### Spotify (Home) — *no-decision personalization, familiar → discovery*
- **Home shows:** most-personalized at the top (familiar playlists), grading into discovery below; designed for **split-second glances**, "content first."
- **JTBD:** "Give me something great *right now* without making me choose."
- **Transfers:** the **familiar→discovery gradient** (start with who/what you trust, ease into new); the "no decision required, just press play" ideal — a strong contrast model for a *decision* home.
- **Does NOT transfer:** pure **algorithmic** personalization with no human name — the literal opposite of Vouch's "a name, never a black box" (§2). Useful as the *anti-pattern* that sharpens Vouch's identity.

## Engagement & habit

### Strava — *recognition feed for your people*
- **Home shows:** a feed of your people's activities; **kudos** (one-tap recognition); progress bars.
- **JTBD:** "Log my effort, be seen by my people, feel momentum."
- **Transfers:** **lightweight recognition** and **momentum/progress** as retention; a feed *of people you actually know* (not strangers) is tolerable trust.
- **Does NOT transfer:** kudos == likes, and it's a feed — both banned (§2). The principle to keep: **utility-based social proof** ("3 you follow saved this", "your guide was used 4× this week" — §5), not vanity hearts.

### Duolingo — *the streak as the day-2 return trigger*
- **Home shows:** the path + the streak; one small daily action.
- **JTBD:** "Make a little progress toward a goal, every day."
- **Transfers:** answers v1's biggest open question — **a return trigger decoupled from the low-frequency core act.** Streak is *separated* from the goal so a single tiny act ("one capture a day") keeps it alive; loss-aversion drives return.
- **Does NOT transfer:** heavy gamification would **cheapen the sacred vouch** (a name-on-it is not an XP token). Borrow the *separated lightweight daily act*, not the cartoon pressure.

### BeReal — *unlock-on-contribute (the cold-start + supply fuse)*
- **Home shows:** nothing until you post — friends' content is **blurred/locked until you contribute**; a once-a-day prompted moment.
- **JTBD:** "A low-pressure, authentic daily moment with my people."
- **Transfers:** **the single most useful idea for Vouch's cold-start** — *you must put a name down to unlock what your people vouched.* It fuses the mandatory-first-vouch (§6), supply, and the consume-after-produce ethic. Plus **prompted-moment** as a return trigger and built-in scarcity/exclusivity.
- **Does NOT transfer:** a rigid daily cadence fights food's natural low frequency — make the prompt **occasion-triggered** (§5: Friday decision), not clockwork.

## Decision, trust & status

### CRED — *one assisted best action + membership status (India)*
- **Home shows:** an **ABA (Assisted Best Action)** card — the single most valuable next action, two taps; premium, members-only feel (credit score 750+).
- **JTBD:** "Do the one valuable thing now, and feel like I'm in a club worth being in."
- **Transfers:** (1) **one confident assisted action on the home** — the model for "Home = where should I go?" as a single trusted answer, not a wall. (2) **Membership / exclusivity / status** maps directly to Vouch's invite-only, "a seat at the table." (3) Premium dark aesthetic; **Indian high-trust-brand** playbook (Shah's home turf).
- **Does NOT transfer:** CRED's best action is a *known transaction* (pay bill); Vouch's is a *recommendation that must earn trust* via a name + a why. The action card must carry a Receipt, not just a CTA.

### Partiful — *link-first artifacts as the product surface*
- **Home shows:** event pages you create + share by link; you don't need the app to RSVP.
- **JTBD:** "Rally my people around a moment."
- **Transfers:** **link-first shareable artifacts as distribution** (Vouch's guide/palate cards, §5: "win the group chat"); an unapologetic exclusivity tone ("for hot people").
- **Does NOT transfer:** event-centric, not a daily home.

### "What to eat tonight" decider apps (Food-Decider, Food Flip, ChooseMy.Food)
- **Home shows:** one fast answer — a spin, a swipe-bracket, an "I don't know what I want" button; **no accounts, no forms, pure fast clarity (<30s).**
- **JTBD:** "End the indecision *right now*."
- **Transfers:** **speed to a single answer**, the playful escape-hatch button, the **bracket/pairwise** mechanic (echoes Beli) to choose without numbers.
- **Does NOT transfer:** they're **random / zero-trust** — the exact void Vouch fills. The answer must have a *name on it*; that's the entire difference.

## Cross-cutting patterns (the transferable kit)
1. **Retroactive supply at cold-start** (Beli, Letterboxd, Foursquare-tastes): seed the home by logging the *past* or declaring tastes — beats cold-start with zero network. Vouch already half-does this ("vouch places you already love").
2. **Unlock-on-contribute** (BeReal): you produce before you consume — fuses cold-start + supply + the mandatory first vouch.
3. **One assisted best action** (CRED, decider apps): the home can be a *single confident answer with a Receipt*, not a card wall. Directly serves §7 "Home = where should I go?".
4. **Situation-first** (Infatuation "Perfect For" == Vouch occasions): decide by life-moment, not cuisine.
5. **A number about people, not places** (Beli taste-similarity == Vouch taste-match %): the only honest number.
6. **Familiar → discovery gradient** (Spotify): open with who you trust, ease into new.
7. **Utility-based recognition, not likes** (Strava-principle minus the hearts): "your guide was used 4×", "3 you follow saved this" (§5).
8. **A separated, lightweight daily act for return** (Duolingo): decouple the return trigger from the low-frequency vouch.
9. **Link-first artifacts as distribution** (Partiful, Letterboxd lists): the guide/palate card *is* the growth surface (§5).

## What explicitly does NOT transfer (the Constitution guardrails)
- **No numeric place score** (kills Beli's 0–10, all star ratings) — §2.
- **No scroll-feed of strangers** (Letterboxd/Strava-style feed) — §2; only people you follow, only with a Receipt.
- **No likes/kudos vanity, no leaderboard coins/stickers** (Strava hearts, Swarm gamification) — §2 vanity ban.
- **No pure-algorithm "trust me" recs** (Spotify model) — §2 black-box ban; every rec carries a *name*.
- **No map-first UI as the product** (§2) — maps are context, not the home.
