# Delight, motion & easter eggs — where they earn their place

> Document only. The rule (Constitution §7): **delight from meaning, never random
> motion.** Every beat below reinforces the one thing Vouch is about — *a name on a
> place, trusted taste, the right moment* — and is concentrated at the **activation
> moments** that drive retention/sharing, not sprinkled everywhere. All
> reduced-motion safe; none on data-dense or decision screens where it'd distract.

## The four "signature" motions (a small, reusable vocabulary)
These recur so the product feels like one thing, and each *means* something:
1. **The stamp lands.** Putting your name down = a press with weight (a subtle
   thunk; haptic on mobile), the VOUCHED stamp settling onto the card. *Why:* the
   vouch is the sacred act; it should feel consequential, not like a form submit.
2. **The pin ignites.** A new vouch lights up on your map with a one-time pulse,
   then settles. *Why:* makes "my taste is becoming a place in the city" visible —
   the core single-player payoff.
3. **The fly-in (borrow).** Follow a palate → their pins *fly* onto your map. *Why:*
   you literally see trust enrich your map — the whole point of following.
4. **The archetype assembles.** Your title ("The Midnight Forager") composes from
   your own vouches. *Why:* earned identity, computed from what you backed — not a
   quiz; the aha of "the product *gets* me" in <60s.

## By surface & stage (where the aha actually is)

### Onboarding (J1) — the highest-leverage delight in the product
- **Each add:** stamp lands + pin ignites on the forming map; counter ticks. *Aha:*
  "my map is becoming real." This is the activation moment — invest most here.
- **The reveal:** the archetype assembles; the shareable palate card builds. *Aha:*
  identity + a thing worth sharing in 60s.
- **First follow:** the fly-in. *Aha:* "their spots just joined mine."

### Add-a-vouch (J2)
- The stamp's weight + the integrity nudge appearing only when earned. *Why:* the
  cost (a sentence) *is* the trust; the motion underlines the gravity, restraint
  signals quality.

### Home / Producer's (when un-parked)
- The territory **lights up** as pins land; occasion-lens dim/lift; "Focus tonight's
  call" halo pulse (built). *Why:* the map *responds* to your intent.
- **Easter egg — the After-Dark hour:** after ~10pm the map subtly warms / deepens
  (the Forager's hour). *Why:* the brand is literally "After Dark," and it's *true*
  (it's about when you eat) — time-aware, not gimmick. Reduced-motion = static warm.

### Palate (J4)
- Follow → fly-in (signature #3) + the shared-occasions line drawing in. *Why:* the
  borrow is the surface's entire purpose; show it landing.

### Guide (J3)
- Build → the artifact assembles live (built). Share → a "sent" stamp.
- **Easter egg — earned milestones:** when a guide crosses a borrow count
  ("borrowed 10×"), a quiet *stamp*, not confetti. *Why:* the Knower's metagame
  reward, tied to **utility** (someone used it), never vanity — exactly the line
  §2 draws.

### Spot
- The **receipt draws its trail** — a hairline connector from the voucher's avatar
  to the name as the page settles. *Why:* makes "this reached you *through* Meera"
  legible as motion, the one differentiator visualized.

### Share / Landing
- The slot-machine "pull for a vouch" (built) is a real toy that teaches the
  premise. Keep; it earns attention on the cold entry.

## A few earned easter eggs (sparing, never on the nose)
- **"The usual."** Vouch the same occasion enough and a dry wink appears in your
  palate ("late-night — undefeated since 2019"). *Why:* recognition of a real habit;
  voice, not noise.
- **The Founding seal.** Early/invited users carry a subtle "admit one" founding
  stamp on their palate. *Why:* status the invite-only brand promises; a reason to
  have been early.
- **Milestone stamps** (1st / 10th / 50th vouch) — a small ledger stamp, not a
  badge wall. *Why:* the diary metagame, on-brand (stamps, not gamification).

## Where delight must NOT go
No confetti, no bouncing loaders, no motion on the decide-tonight answer or any
dense list, no sound by default. If a beat doesn't make *trust, taste, or the
moment* more felt, it's decoration — cut it. Everything honors `prefers-reduced-
motion` (the meaning survives as a static state).

## The principle, one line
Motion is how Vouch says *"your name is on this, and it matters"* — so it belongs
exactly where a name goes on a place, where taste becomes identity, and where trust
travels to you. Nowhere else.
